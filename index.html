Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how FraudArchive may collect, use, store, display, and protect information when users interact with the FraudArchive Discord bot, reporting systems, database, or related services.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on how FraudArchive is used, we may process information such as:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Reported Discord accounts
  • Reporter Discord accounts
  • Report descriptions and reasons
  • Evidence submitted with reports
  • Relevant links or attachments
  • Appeal and correction information
  • Moderation notes and decisions
  • Timestamps
  • Security and abuse-prevention logs

3. Sensitive Information

Users should not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information, including passwords, payment card information, home addresses, phone numbers, government identification documents, private financial information, or unrelated private information.

FraudArchive may remove, restrict, or redact this information when discovered.

4. Why We Process Information

Information may be processed to:

  • Operate and maintain FraudArchive.
  • Receive and review reports.
  • Help users identify previously reported accounts.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, or malicious reporting.
  • Review appeals and correction requests.
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service.
  • Enforce FraudArchive rules and prevent misuse.

5. Information Displayed to Users

Some information connected to reviewed reports may be shown to FraudArchive users. This may include Discord account identifiers, usernames, report reasons, report status, relevant evidence or evidence summaries, and report dates.

FraudArchive aims to limit displayed information to what is reasonably necessary for the purpose of the service.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Fraud-related information may be retained while it remains relevant for fraud prevention, moderation, appeals, abuse prevention, or maintaining the integrity of FraudArchive.

Information may be corrected, restricted, archived, anonymized, or deleted when appropriate.

7. Sharing of Information

FraudArchive does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by services required to operate FraudArchive, such as hosting, database, infrastructure, or Discord-related services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect FraudArchive, its users, or others from serious abuse or unlawful activity.

8. Appeals, Corrections, and Privacy Requests

If you believe information relating to you is false, inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or misleading, you may request a review through the official FraudArchive Discord server.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights relating to personal information about you, including requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about how your data is used.

FraudArchive may request reasonable verification before processing a request.

9. Security

FraudArchive uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect stored information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Third-Party Services

FraudArchive may rely on third-party services, including Discord and infrastructure or hosting providers. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when FraudArchive changes or when legal, security, or operational requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy requests, appeals, corrections, reports, or other concerns, contact FraudArchive through the official Discord server: discord.gg/e3NpMn3BHA.
