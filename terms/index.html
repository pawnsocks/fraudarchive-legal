2. Purpose of FraudArchive

FraudArchive is a community safety and information service intended to help users review reports relating to suspected scams, fraud, impersonation, or similar harmful activity.

A FraudArchive record does not automatically prove that a person committed fraud. Reports may include claims submitted by third parties. Users should review the available information and make their own decisions.

3. Reports

Reports must be submitted honestly and should include relevant information or evidence where possible. Knowingly false, manipulated, misleading, malicious, or abusive reports are prohibited.

FraudArchive must not be used to harass, threaten, impersonate, defame, dox, or intentionally target another person.

4. Evidence and Submitted Content

Users should only submit information reasonably necessary to explain or support a report. Unnecessary sensitive information should not be submitted.

  • Passwords or authentication codes
  • Payment card information
  • Home addresses
  • Government identification documents
  • Private financial information
  • Unrelated private or personal information

FraudArchive may remove, restrict, or redact content that is unnecessary, sensitive, unsafe, unlawful, or unrelated to a report.

Records may be corrected, updated, restricted, archived, or removed when new information becomes available.

6. Appeals and Corrections

If you believe a record about you is false, inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or misleading, you may request a review through the official FraudArchive Discord server.

Appeals should explain the issue clearly and may include evidence supporting the request. Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that a record will be changed or removed.
